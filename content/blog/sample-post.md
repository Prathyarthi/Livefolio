A resume is a short summary. A portfolio is a place where the work itself can sit next to the summary, so a reader can look at the evidence instead of taking your word for it.

## Why a portfolio helps

A portfolio can hold things a one-page resume has no room for:

- Projects with a short description of what you did and why it mattered
- Writing and articles you have published elsewhere
- Links to profiles that show your work over time
- A clear, shareable address you can put in an application

## How Livefolio fits in

Livefolio turns your resume PDF into a portfolio, lets you connect sources like GitHub, Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode, LeetCode, Stack Overflow and Codeforces, and publishes the result on your own livefolio.me address. It works for any profession, since a portfolio is just a well organised page about your work.
